PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: January 2026

At PK Entertainment (“we”, “us”, “our”), your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or engage our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and city when you submit an enquiry or book a consultation.
  • Project details such as room dimensions, budget range, timelines and design preferences.
  • Correspondence records when you call, email or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type and pages visited, collected through standard analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, deliver and support projects, process payments, and improve our services. With your consent, we may also send occasional updates about projects, products or offers. You can opt out at any time.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited details with trusted partners — such as installation teams, equipment suppliers or logistics providers — strictly to deliver your project. These partners are required to protect your data and use it only for the agreed purpose. We may also disclose information where required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain project and contact records for as long as needed to provide services, honour warranties and meet legal or accounting obligations. Enquiry data that does not result in a project is typically retained for up to 24 months.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent to marketing communications. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@pkentertainment.in.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
