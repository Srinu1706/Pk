Our story

BUILT ON A SIMPLE BELIEF

PK Entertainment began with a frustration: too many "home theaters" were really just a projector and a loud speaker in a bare room. The technology was there. The experience was not.

We set out to do it differently — to treat the room as an instrument, to design for the way sound actually behaves, and to make every technical decision serve the feeling of being in a real cinema. Six years and 250+ projects later, that belief still drives everything we do.

Today our work spans private homes, boutique multiplexes, auditoriums, boardrooms and studios. The scale changes; the standard does not.
