REFUND POLICY

Last updated: January 2026

We want every PK Entertainment purchase to feel considered and fair. This policy explains when refunds, returns and cancellations apply. It should be read together with your project agreement or order confirmation.

1. Standard Products

Unused, uninstalled products in original packaging may be returned within 7 days of delivery for a refund, minus shipping and handling costs. To be eligible, items must be undamaged and accompanied by proof of purchase. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days of inspection.

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom-made furniture, seating, acoustic panels and made-to-order items.
  • Installed equipment and products with broken seals or used consumables.
  • Calibration, design, consultation and installation services already performed.
  • Clearance or demonstration items marked as final sale.

3. Damaged or Defective Items

If a product arrives damaged or develops a defect within its warranty period, notify us within 48 hours of delivery (or immediately upon discovery of a defect). We will arrange inspection, repair or replacement in line with the manufacturer's warranty. Photographs and order details help us resolve issues faster.

4. Project Cancellations

Project cancellations must be made in writing. Because each project involves design time, procurement and scheduling, cancellation charges apply based on the stage reached:

  • Before design sign-off: any refundable advance is returned after deducting design and site-visit costs incurred.
  • After design sign-off, before procurement: design fees and any committed third-party costs are non-refundable.
  • After procurement or installation begins: custom and special-order items, labour and committed costs are non-refundable.

5. Order Cancellation by Us

In the rare event that we cannot fulfil an order due to stock, serviceability or technical reasons, we will inform you promptly and offer an alternative or a full refund of amounts paid for the affected item.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are made via the original payment method. Bank processing times may add 5–7 business days. Any applicable taxes, transaction fees or logistics charges already incurred may be deducted as permitted by law.

7. Warranty Support

Refunds are separate from warranty service. Defective products covered by warranty will be repaired or replaced according to the manufacturer's terms. Please retain your invoice and handover documentation.

8. How to Request a Refund

Email hello@pkentertainment.in with your order or project reference, reason for the request and supporting photographs where relevant. Our team will acknowledge your request within 2 business days.
