SHIPPING & DELIVERY

Last updated: January 2026

PK Entertainment supplies and installs cinema seating, audio, projection, acoustic and automation products across India. Because most purchases are part of a designed project, delivery and installation are usually coordinated together.

1. Delivery Coverage

We deliver to serviceable pin codes across India through our logistics partners and installation teams. Remote locations may require additional transit time or a site-specific delivery charge, which will be confirmed before order acceptance.

2. Dispatch Timelines

  • In-stock accessories and equipment: dispatched within 3–7 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Large equipment and furniture: typically 2–4 weeks, depending on brand and model availability.
  • Custom-made seating, acoustic panels and joinery: typically 4–8 weeks from design approval.

Exact lead times are confirmed in your quotation or order confirmation.

3. Delivery Charges

Delivery, transit insurance and installation are quoted separately unless included in your project package. Charges depend on product size, weight, destination and access conditions (stairs, lift availability, parking distance).

4. Receiving Your Order

Please inspect all packages at the time of delivery. Note any visible damage on the delivery receipt and photograph it where possible. Report shortages or damage within 48 hours at hello@pkentertainment.in so we can resolve it quickly.

5. Installation

For project purchases, delivery is followed by professional installation and calibration by our team or an authorised partner. Installation dates are scheduled in coordination with you and may depend on site readiness, power availability and civil work completion.

6. Access and Site Requirements

Please ensure clear access for delivery teams, working power, and safe conditions on site. Additional visits caused by inaccessible sites or incomplete preparation may incur charges.

7. Delays

While we work hard to meet every date, delays can occur due to manufacturer backorders, customs, weather, transport disruptions or events beyond our control. We will keep you informed and adjust the schedule as quickly as possible.

8. International Orders

We currently deliver and install within India. For international enquiries, please contact us to discuss feasibility; import duties, taxes and compliance are the buyer's responsibility unless agreed otherwise.
